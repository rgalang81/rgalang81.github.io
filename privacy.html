Privacy policy: PreviouslyPH uploader

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The PreviouslyPH uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Previously, in the Philippines. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists, schedules and comments.

What it does not do

It does not collect, store, sell or share data about viewers or any other Google user. It does not access any account other than the owner's channel.

Storage

The sign-in token is kept in a private settings store used only by the owner. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
